# Risk tiers + AC observability: decision & objective quality lenses (v1.13.1)

## Test Reference

`tests/test_risk.py` and `tests/test_ac_quality.py`

## Scope

Backfilled verification contract for STORY-626 ("Decision & objective quality
— computed risk tiers + AC observability, v1.13.1"). Two honest-quality
lenses, both accounting-not-policing.

### Risk tiers

- `src/specflow/lib/risk.py` — deterministic tier compute (T0/T1/T2) from
  reversibility + blast-radius-count; pure read of the artifact graph.
- `src/specflow/commands/risk_tier.py` — `specflow risk-tier <IDs>` renders
  the tier (read-only; the tier gates nothing).
- `tests/test_risk.py` — exercises tier floor, blast-radius counting, and
  read-only dispatch.

### AC observability

- `src/specflow/lib/ac_quality.py` — classifies AC items as
  **observable** / **aspirational** / **unclassified**. The anti-cry-wolf
  guardrail is the conjunction: aspirational ONLY with no outcome marker AND
  an ambiguity match; a miss demotes to unclassified (info), never aspirational.
- `tests/test_ac_quality.py` — exercises the conjunction guardrail and the
  observable/aspirational/unclassified classifier.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `uv run pytest tests/test_risk.py tests/test_ac_quality.py -q` exits 0
   (recorded via `specflow verify`).
2. `specflow risk-tier <IDs>` renders a deterministic tier and exits without
   mutating artifacts.
3. AC observability classifies without smearing domain observables as
   aspirational (conjunction guardrail), and never produces a blocking
   finding.
