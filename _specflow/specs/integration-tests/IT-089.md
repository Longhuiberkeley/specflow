tests/test_ci_gate_history.py builds a throwaway git repository with distinct commit authors and runs the real run_ci_gate and check_independence. A commit is a snapshot, not a CLI step, so a commit's step is legal when a chain of single legal transitions reaches it and every policy-gated status on that chain is charged to that commit's author. H1 PerStepLegality: a stuttering approved->cancelled->implemented history (net pair legal, hidden step unreachable) and a one-commit verified->approved are flagged with 'illegal transition'; a one-commit draft->implemented by a non-approver is flagged for the 'approved' it passed through; a one-commit approved->verified by an authorised reviewer (cascade-status) passes (DEF-016). H2 PerStepAuthority: draft->approved by a non-approver hidden behind a reviewer's later verification is flagged against the approving commit's author; a one-commit draft->verified by a reviewer is flagged for 'approved'; a legitimate approver/implementer/reviewer PR passes; an unrelated older commit by the approver does not lend its role; a rename inside the PR is walked across. H3 IndependenceAcrossRename: an implementer who authored REQ-FOO-ab12.md before renumber-drafts renamed it to REQ-001.md is still an implementer (library and CI level), while an outsider may verify. Also pins the solo-mode no-op, the no-change message and the missing-ref exit code. On the pre-fix code 8 of 12 failed, reproducing DEF-002 (H1/H2) and DEF-003 (H3).
