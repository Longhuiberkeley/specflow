# Accounting not policing: surface, never block; warnings never fail a commit or audit

## Practice
SpecFlow records and surfaces state; the human decides. Warnings (stale docs, suspect artifacts, audit concerns) are advisory — they never fail a commit hook and never escalate an audit exit code. Blocking is reserved for the explicit human approval gate (draft→approved).

## Rationale
A tool that blocks on every warning trains users to ignore all warnings (cry-wolf) or to bypass the tool. Accounting-with-pointers keeps the human in authority while making problems visible; policing erodes trust and gets disabled.

## Verification
The commit hook (`.specflow` pre-commit) exits non-zero ONLY on hard structural errors, never on a warning. `specflow project-audit` returns 0 even with concerns listed. No skill coerces a status transition without human confirmation.
