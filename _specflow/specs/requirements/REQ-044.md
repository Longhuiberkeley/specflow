## Rationale
The best-practice subsystem is half-wired: generated BPs are linkless and dedup-disabled (commands/handbook.py:53-55), hand-authored doctrine lives in an orphaned reference, matching is tag-intersection only (lib/ci.py:33-37), and nothing validates anatomy or lineage. A uniform, provenance-annotated spine is the foundation for binding, self-generation, and standards alignment.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a BP created after migration, when rendered, then it SHALL carry the five sections Practice / Applies when / Work products / Verification / Rationale.
2. Given a BP with provenance metadata, when specflow practices validate runs, then anatomy, verification-method taxonomy membership, provenance-source resolvability, and supersession lineage SHALL be checked deterministically with zero LLM inference.
3. Given a legacy BP without provenance, when specflow practices migrate runs, then it SHALL stamp provenance idempotently, preview via --dry-run without writing, and fold into refresh/init/apply_pack.
4. Given the transitional release, when BP statuses are evaluated, then the map SHALL be draft -> approved -> superseded with approved: [draft, active] and superseded: [approved, active], pinned byte-identical in pytest and shipped atomically with the applicability-first rewrite of load_active_best_practices.
5. Given bundled practices, when listed, then fixed seed identifiers SHALL exist in lib/practices_seed.py with applicability predicates, with specflow handbook generate kept as a deprecated alias.
