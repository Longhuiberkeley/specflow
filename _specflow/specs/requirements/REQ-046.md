## Rationale
Autoresearch must be a complete, self-improving researcher: width/depth steering is prose-only today, EDA is mandated but never taught (the referenced EDA checks #1/#4 do not exist), results analysis never feeds ideation, and adjacent-field wisdom has no live import path.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a COMP with logged EXPTs, when specflow autoresearch frontier runs, then per-lineage depth (noise-adjusted) and per-COMP width SHALL be computed zero-token from existing frontmatter, with stagnation states (depth exhausted; breadth without depth), an advisory move menu (switch formulation, restart with memory, landscape re-survey), and no count-based rotation.
2. Given a LOOP in a domain with EDA lenses, when EDA completes, then EDA accounting SHALL be applicability-aware, reporting not-applicable for domains without lenses and offering an agenda-revision advisory only where lenses exist.
3. Given saved train or out-of-fold diagnostics for the current best, when ideating, then error and results analysis SHALL be treated as a first-class phase (prediction-vs-truth slicing, residual inspection; P&L attribution and regime tags for strategies), documented in pack references, with eval-partition data never read.
4. Given an analysis-only iteration, when logged, then it SHALL be an anchored no_op EXPT (change_category analysis, research_progress present) exempt from every evidence-free streak window.
5. Given a completed LOOP, when planning the next, then autoresearch plan --inherit SHALL machine-seed the agenda from unexplored_directions, open entries, and the latest condensation brief.
6. Given ideation or a landscape re-survey, when the agent consults guidance, then an adjacent-field practice lens (Kaggle, quant, experiment design) SHALL be available through practices list --scope framing.
7. Given LOOP completion, when review runs, then a condensation brief SHALL be required, with status output ranked and capped to the top three actionable signals.
