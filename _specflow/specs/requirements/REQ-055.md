## Rationale
No standards pack has ever been validated against a real standard: a clause counts as covered when any artifact carries a complies_with link whose string equals the clause id, clauses have no strength or applicability, pack-validate checks only that keys exist, and init cannot resolve a project-local pack. Whether bring-your-own-standard works is an open empirical question that must be answered with numbers before it is claimed.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a pack clause, when it is authored, then it MAY declare parent, strength (shall, should, may), applies_when, requires templates (exists_linked, exists_verified_test, independent_approver, ordered_before) and page_ref.
2. Given a clause that cannot be encoded as requires templates, when the pack is validated, then the clause SHALL be marked judgment so that it never passes silently.
3. Given the pack-validate command, when it runs, then it SHALL check clause id uniqueness and format, parent existence, the strength enum, and a completeness cross-check of clause-id regex hits in the supplied source text against extracted clauses.
4. Given compliance coverage, when it is reported, then covered SHALL mean all requires templates discharged, with the bare-link count reported separately as claimed.
5. Given init with a preset name or refresh with packs, when a project-local pack directory of that name exists, then the local pack SHALL be resolved before the bundled directory.
6. Given the pilot, when it runs against NASA NPR 7150.2D (public) and a private local ASPICE 4.0 SWE.1-6 pack (never committed), then it SHALL publish M1 clause extraction recall and precision against a hand-built gold set of at least 30 clauses, M2 seeded-mutation detection rate over at least 50 mutations, M3 false-positive rate on a known-good corpus, M4 an independent adequacy audit of 20 covered clauses, and M5 an expressiveness census.
7. Given the pilot results, when they are recorded, then they SHALL live in a SPIKE with results in an AUD and a DEC.
8. Given the README and pack descriptions, until the pilot has published its numbers, then they SHALL NOT state or imply standards compliance.
