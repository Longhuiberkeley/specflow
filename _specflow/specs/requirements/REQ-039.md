# COMP lifecycle: closure gate, reversible pause, closure accounting

## Rationale
COMP closure (active → completed) is the only consequential autoresearch transition with no criteria, no protocol text, and no human gate; agents close COMPs prematurely in practice. The paused status is a one-way door (paused → active is impossible in competition.yaml), stranding competitions permanently. Closure signals (goals, confirmed FINDs, open agenda directions) are computed nowhere deterministically.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. COMP completion requires every COMP.goals entry either satisfied-with-confirmed-FIND-evidence or explicitly abandoned-with-reason, plus direct-user confirmation (no self-approval, mirroring the FIND gate).
2. artifact-lint warns on a completed COMP with zero confirmed FINDs, resolving FIND→COMP via both frontmatter competition and belongs_to links.
3. paused → active is a legal transition while COMP creation still lands on active, via an initial_statuses schema key honored by implicit-status resolution and the creation sanction gate.
4. specflow autoresearch status renders a COMP-level closure-readiness block (confirmed FIND count, open agenda directions, goals echo) independent of LOOP resolution — including when all LOOPs are completed/plateaued.
