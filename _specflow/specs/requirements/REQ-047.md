## Rationale
A single gameable number invites loss hacking: within-noise jumps, guard regressions, and evaluator drift are undetectable today.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given COMP setup, when the evaluation harness is frozen, then an evaluator_fingerprint (verify command plus evaluation-script hashes) SHALL be recorded.
2. Given an EXPT logged under a changed fingerprint, when artifact-lint runs, then it SHALL flag once and route to the documented successor-COMP path, never per-EXPT and never retroactively.
3. Given a single-iteration primary-metric jump, when status runs, then jumps exceeding a noise-probe-denominated threshold SHALL be flagged mechanism-explanation-required (advisory).
4. Given an EXPT with guard metrics, when a primary gain coincides with a guard regression beyond noise, then status SHALL warn.
5. Given an EXPT with an external score, when an external leaderboard exists, then the CV-external relation SHALL be rendered with inconsistent gains demoted, falling back to guard metrics plus trial-count-deflated metrics when no leaderboard exists.
6. Given COMP setup in quant domains, when the setup protocol runs, then a metric bundle with a fixed horizon SHALL be required, not a single number.
