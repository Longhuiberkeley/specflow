## Rationale
Guidance must actively shape lifecycle artifacts and be auditable: today no consumer reports BP adherence, ARCH/STORY shaping is unverifiable, and complies_with is never read.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an in-scope practice and a REQ, ARCH, or STORY, when linked guided_by, then the edge SHALL be schema-legal (requirement.yaml allowed_link_roles; role_targets rows for ARCH and STORY) and rendered by trace.
2. Given approved in-scope BPs, when specflow brief runs, then bound and unbound counts SHALL be reported for REQ, ARCH, and STORY.
3. Given a REQ citing a BP via guided_by, when artifact-lint runs, then the single evidence-based check bp-application SHALL verify graph facts only (link presence, field values, coverage arithmetic, test-result status), warning-first with opt-in lint.bp_evidence_strict, skipping BPs lacking provenance and REQs unmodified since BP approval.
4. Given a mandatory or recommended practice dropped by a project, when the drop is recorded, then tailoring SHALL cite an approved DEC.
5. Given a guided_by edge inside a trace chain, when chain depth is computed, then its treatment SHALL match the documented decision (depth-neutral or one hop).
6. Given body-text criteria in a BP Verification section, when checks run, then they SHALL render as advisory inspection items, never compiled into predicates.
