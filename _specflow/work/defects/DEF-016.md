# CI gate rejects a legal multi-step commit: cascade-status approved to verified in one commit is flagged illegal

The STORY-698 per-commit walk compared commit snapshots against single allowed_status edges, but one commit often records several legal CLI steps (cascade-status legal_walk, or two update calls before a commit). Fix: a commit step is legal when a chain of single legal transitions reaches it (BFS over allowed_status); every policy-gated status on that chain is authorised against the commit's author, so a one-commit draft -> implemented by a non-approver still fails on 'approved'.
