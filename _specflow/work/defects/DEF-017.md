# dead-oracle warnings escalate to blocking after three full artifact-lint runs

The dead-oracle check (STORY-699) emits warnings only, but the STORY-663 escalation turns any warning seen in three consecutive full runs into a blocker unless the check is exempt. Fix: add dead-oracle to the escalation exemption next to bp-application and fingerprint-drift.
