## Risk
The lifecycle collapse and the applicability-first loader rewrite must ship in the same release (splitting them strands refreshed installs and dogfood mirrors), and practices migrate must land one release before bp-application and bp-verification checks activate, or the 3-run warn-to-blocking escalation (artifact_lint.py:35) could fire on unmigrated legacy artifacts.

## Likelihood / Impact
Medium likelihood (sequencing pressure to ship increments independently) / High impact (broken installs, false-blocking lint).

## Mitigation
Pin both couplings in the increment-1 and increment-2 STORY acceptance criteria; fold practices migrate into refresh/init/apply_pack; add pytest coverage asserting checks skip BPs without provenance; keep --sanctioned and standards-gaps removal behind one-release aliases.
