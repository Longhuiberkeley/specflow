# V-pair check requires the paired test type (REQ-013)

## Decision
`find_missing_v_pairs` (the SPEC-anchored REQ-013 / ARCH-008 metric) counts a spec as paired only when a test of its **paired type** (`V_MODEL_PAIRS`: REQ to QT, ARCH to IT, DDD to UT) is linked by `verified_by` in either legal shape: the test's link to the spec, or the spec's own outgoing link to the test. A `verified_by` from any other artifact (a STORY, a DEC, a wrong-level test) no longer pairs the spec. The returned tuple now carries the paired **test** prefix, matching the docstring; `artifact-lint --type links` renders "REQ-001 (no QT verification)" instead of "(no REQ verification)".

This supersedes the docstring note "intentionally coexist — do not merge or fix" only for the verifier-type laxness. The two REQ-012 metrics (STORY-anchored `check_coverage`, SPEC-anchored `find_missing_v_pairs`) stay distinct and are not merged.

## Metric delta (REQ-013)
Same dogfood snapshot (922 artifacts), pre-change vs post-change function:

| Missing V-pairs | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Total | 8 | 11 |
| REQ (no QT) | 6 | 6 |
| ARCH (no IT) | 1 | 3 |
| DDD (no UT) | 1 | 2 |

Newly reported: ARCH-032 and ARCH-033 (verified only by UT-084, a unit test) and DDD-029 (verified only by IT-049, an integration test). None became newly paired. `specflow status` missing-pairs count moves by the same +3.

## Consequences
- Wrong-level verification links now surface as missing pairs; fixing them means linking the paired test type, not relabelling.
- The spec-held `verified_by` shape now counts, so authors can hold the link on either end.
