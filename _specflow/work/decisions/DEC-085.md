## Decision
Every best-practice artifact carries one five-part anatomy (Practice / Applies when / Work products / Verification / Rationale) with optional provenance (bundled | standard | synthesized | learned), source, confidence, strength, applicability, tailoring, and verification_method. The status lifecycle collapses to draft -> approved -> superseded; the transitional map (approved: [draft, active]; superseded: [approved, active]) ships atomically with the applicability-first rewrite of lib/ci.load_active_best_practices. Bundled practices move to lib/practices_seed.py with stable seed ids; handbook generate remains a deprecated alias. This lifts the DEC-084 deferral of the BP-surface gaps: the inert applies_to field is superseded by the applicability-first loader, and the promote lane is handled by the flywheel decision (DEC-087).

## Rationale
Creation is bifurcated (generated BPs linkless and dedup-disabled at commands/handbook.py:53-55; hand-authored doctrine in an orphaned reference), matching is tag-intersection only (lib/ci.py:33-37), and nothing validates anatomy or lineage. A uniform spine is the foundation REQ-045 through REQ-051 build on.

## Consequences
Migration is additive and idempotent (practices migrate --dry-run); the collapse plus loader rewrite must not be split across releases; legacy BPs keep working. practices validate checks anatomy, taxonomy membership, provenance resolvability, and supersession lineage deterministically.
