## Decision
The REQ-side guided_by -> best-practice link is the binding spine (additive to requirement.yaml allowed_link_roles plus a new requirement/guided_by row in role_targets.py); BP-side applies_to stays a supplement for BP-declared scope. Exactly one evidence-based check (bp-application, modeled on compliance-evidence) reports adherence for REQ, ARCH, and STORY; brief reports bound and unbound counts. Compiled verification is restricted to graph facts (link presence, field values, coverage arithmetic, test-result status); body-text criteria render as advisory inspection items. Warning-first with opt-in lint.bp_evidence_strict, backfill grace for REQs unmodified since BP approval, and a migration stamp so checks skip BPs lacking provenance. Dropping a mandatory or recommended practice requires tailoring citing an approved DEC (ISO-26262-style). The guided_by chain-depth treatment (depth-neutral versus one hop; _CHAIN_DEPTH_ROLES at artifacts.py:942-943) must be decided and documented, not left to drift.

## Rationale
BPs cannot be shown to have shaped a REQ today: project-audit has zero best-practice reads and complies_with is never read. A presence-only warn was rejected as link-farming bait; keyword-overlap compilation was rejected as manufactured assurance.

## Consequences
Architecture and story shaping become auditable (edges already legal via role_targets.py:65,80); accounting, not policing.
