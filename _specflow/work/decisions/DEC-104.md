# Fold framing into /specflow-discover; narrow the bring-your-own-ideation positioning

## Context

docs/comparison.md ("What SpecFlow expects you to bring") says SpecFlow has no ideation phase by design and sends users to BMAD, gstack `/office-hours` or superpowers brainstorming first. The 2026-10 landscape survey (DEC-103) found that every stack worth borrowing from has a persona-free front door for a fuzzy idea, and that the owner's objection is to role framing and ideation weight, not to framing itself. A three-angle design panel scored "framing as the zero end of /specflow-discover's readiness curve" highest (8.6) against a dedicated `/specflow-frame` skill (7.9) and an explore/stress-test skill pair (6.3), because framing is scale-adaptive rather than a new stage, costs no Python and roughly three router lines, and has no handoff seam.

Owner decision (2026-10-18): fold framing into `/specflow-discover`; no new skill.

## Decision

- SpecFlow frames a fuzzy idea inside `/specflow-discover` (new `references/framing.md`): trigger when at most one required readiness dimension is met; ground in the repository first; one question per message with a recommended answer, capped at five; a premise ledger the user accepts or rejects explicitly; two or three option briefs plus do-nothing; a hard stop; a **go / clarify / kill** verdict recorded as a DEC tagged `framing`; a go verdict continues at discover step 2 with the lean/full exchange count restarted.
- The positioning in docs/comparison.md narrows from "bring your own ideation" to: SpecFlow frames a fuzzy idea into a decision-ready problem and a go/clarify/kill ruling; market validation, pricing and product-market fit remain the user's to bring.
- No new artifact type, field, link role, or skill. REQs born from a go verdict link `guided_by` to the framing DEC. A kill is an approved `Dropped:` DEC so conflict-check re-surfaces it.
- If dogfooding shows hosts miss the discover trigger on a vague opening, `framing.md` moves unchanged into a `/specflow-frame` skill under a new DEC; nothing here precludes that.

## Consequences

- `/specflow-discover`'s description claims fuzzy ideas; `/specflow-start` routes "not sure it's worth building" to discover; README, docs/comparison.md and docs/lifecycle.md say so.
- Framing never creates REQs or code and never self-approves: go and kill DECs are created `--sanctioned` with the user's quoted verdict; clarify stays `draft`.
- Deferred follow-up: a lint rule for a REQ `guided_by` a framing DEC that is still draft or resolved as kill, which ships only with its negative fixture.
