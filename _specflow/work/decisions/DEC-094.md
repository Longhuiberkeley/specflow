# Retire the Gemini CLI host

## Decision
SpecFlow no longer supports Gemini CLI. The `gemini` platform is removed from the platform registry, and the `gemini-toml` skill-export format is removed. `init --platform gemini` and `refresh --platform gemini` fail cleanly and list the available platforms. A project that has only `.gemini/` is treated as having no detected platform. SpecFlow does not migrate existing `.gemini/` or `GEMINI.md` files; owners delete them. Command files that SpecFlow once wrote under `.gemini/commands` are orphaned and harmless. Antigravity was never a registered host. The source scanner keeps `.gemini/` and `.antigravitycli/` in its exclusion list, so those caches are never read as source. The other 13 hosts are unchanged.

## Rationale
The owner does not use or want to maintain Gemini or Antigravity support (2026-09-30). The host produced no value and caused churn: `refresh --all-platforms` wrote a redundant GEMINI.md into projects that already point Gemini at CLAUDE.md through `.gemini/settings.json` and symlink `.gemini/skills` to `.claude/skills`. Carrying an unused host costs test surface, prose in the init skill, and an export format.

## Consequences
- This supersedes the Gemini parts of REQ-001 (platform detection), REQ-005 (Gemini TOML) and ARCH-003 (platform table). Their other content is unchanged.
- The registry holds 13 platforms. Docs and ROADMAP say 13.
- Consumer repos remove GEMINI.md and `.gemini/` by hand (a symlinked `.gemini/skills` is removed as a link, never followed).
