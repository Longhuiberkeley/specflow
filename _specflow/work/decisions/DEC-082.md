# Knowledge lives in the artifact system, not docs/

## Decision

SpecFlow's durable knowledge (standards, methodology, conventions, decisions) is stored as artifacts — DEC, ARCH, DDD, and artifact-attached references — never as free-standing docs/ pages that evolve outside the traceability system.

docs/ becomes at most a derived rendering target (generated from artifacts), not a source of truth. Nothing that instructs models or contributors may live only in docs/.

## Scope

- docs/skill-standards.md content migrates into the artifact system (e.g., a DEC or ARCH reference attached to the skills subsystem).
- The /specflow-doc skill premise (docs are a knowledge surface, not artifacts) is revisited: its role shifts toward maintaining artifact-embedded knowledge and any generated doc output.
- agent-context.md and skill bodies must point at artifact-native knowledge (specflow brief, artifact IDs), never at docs/ paths.

## Consequences

- One knowledge graph: brief/trace/lint cover everything models are told.
- Doc drift becomes detectable (artifact-lint / reconcile on generated surfaces).
- Migration is incremental: highest-traffic docs first; docs/ pages get superseded-by-artifact links as they move.
