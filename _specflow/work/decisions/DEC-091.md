# Coverage credits canonical REQ refined_by ARCH and STORY-to-test verified_by (REQ-012 metric delta)

## Decision
`check_coverage` (the STORY-anchored REQ-012 coverage metric) credits both legal link shapes:
- Architectural refinement: the canonical `REQ refined_by ARCH` (link held by the REQ) **or** the legacy `ARCH derives_from REQ`.
- Test verification of a STORY: the test's `verified_by` to the STORY **or** the STORY's own outgoing `verified_by` to a UT/IT/QT (only test-typed targets count).

A REQ refined only through the legacy derives_from shape produces ONE collapsed accounting-class INFO line (`accounting_count` / `accounting_detail`; project-audit concern `coverage-shape`, registered in `_ACCOUNTING_CONCERNS`, info severity). It is never a warning: it cannot escalate through the STORY-663 persistent-warning counter, cannot drive project-audit exit 2, and cannot mint a CHL. The missing-ARCH warning text is kept byte-identical.

## Metric delta (REQ-012)
Measured on the same dogfood snapshot (922 artifacts) with the pre-change and post-change functions, `specflow artifact-lint --type coverage`:

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage warnings (total) | 50 | 50 |
| Structural (missing ARCH / missing STORY) | 4 | 4 |
| Test-verification gaps | 46 | 46 |
| Approved-story chain coverage | 147/161 | 147/161 |
| Accounting INFO: REQs refined only via legacy derives_from | n/a | 47 |

The warning counts do not move because every dogfood REQ that has the canonical `refined_by` shape (5) also has a legacy `derives_from`, and every STORY-held `verified_by` (178 links) is mirrored by a test-held link. That masking is why the divergence stayed latent. Consumer repos that author only the canonical shapes stop receiving false "no ARCH" and "no UT/IT/QT" warnings. The 4 remaining structural gaps (REQ-054..057) have no ARCH link in either shape.

## Consequences
- A future ontology-driven coverage view (DEC-090) inherits both shapes. The 47-REQ accounting line is the migration backlog toward the canonical shape.
- The CHANGELOG entry for v1.17.0 names this delta.
