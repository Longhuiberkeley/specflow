## Decision
BP is the durable, project-scoped home for research process knowledge; FIND stays COMP-scoped domain evidence with its human confirmation gate, and LOOP frontmatter (lessons_learned, looplevel_findings, unexplored_directions) stays the capture surface. One promotion lane keyed by source type: practices promote --from FIND|PREV|LOOP performs a deterministic graph transform (source exists, idempotent refusal when already cited, derives_from edge, provenance learned, confidence low, applicability from the COMP domain). suggest-finds --process renders recurrence candidates only across distinct COMPs (LOOP ids and quotations); the model judges recurrence and authors the draft; one human gate approves. Learned BPs default to strength informal or recommended. Confidence raises are proposed by the CLI on a structurally independent second confirmation and applied by the human, never auto-mutated.

## Rationale
A kind-tagged process FIND was rejected (inherits the required-competition field and confirm gate, double-gating agile lessons); a new PROCESS artifact type was rejected as type proliferation; LOOP fields alone are write-only today (loop.yaml:21 has no CLI consumer) and invisible cross-COMP, in tension with DEC-082. This makes the adversarial-lenses FIND-to-BP promise real and gives trace a complete EXPT -> FIND -> BP -> REQ chain.

## Consequences
The flywheel is queryable: LOOP setup records consulted in-scope BP ids (mirroring checklists_applied), so next-LOOP consumption is a graph fact rather than prose.
